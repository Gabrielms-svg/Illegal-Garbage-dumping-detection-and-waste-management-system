About Our Project

Building a cleaner, healthier, and sustainable society through technology.

Project Overview

Our project is an intelligent, technology-driven solution designed to combat the growing problem of illegal garbage dumping and inefficient waste management in urban and rural environments. By integrating Machine Learning, Computer Vision, and Web Technologies, the system aims to create a cleaner, healthier, and more sustainable society.

This platform continuously monitors CCTV and live camera feeds to automatically detect illegal dumping activities in public areas. Once a dumping event is identified, the system captures evidence in the form of images and videos, detects the vehicle and number plate, and records the location and time of the incident. These details are securely stored and made available to concerned authorities for further action.

In addition to automated detection, the system also provides a user reporting mechanism, allowing citizens to report garbage dumping incidents through a simple web interface. This encourages community participation in maintaining cleanliness and accountability.

Features & Capabilities

The system further includes:

  • 📍 Hotspot Identification – detects frequent dumping locations using data analytics
  • 🤖 Awareness Chatbot – educates users about proper waste disposal methods
  • 🗺️ Legal Disposal Guidance – guides users to authorized waste disposal sites
  • 📊 Analytics Dashboard – helps authorities analyze trends and take preventive measures

By combining automation, public participation, and data-driven decision-making, this project contributes towards the vision of Smart Cities, Clean India, and Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). It empowers authorities, organizations, and communities to work together in building a cleaner and greener future.
